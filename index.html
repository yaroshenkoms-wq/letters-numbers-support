ABC Tracing: Learn to Write — Support

A free app that teaches children to recognise, say and write letters and numbers. No ads, no purchases, works offline.

How do I change the language?

Menu language: buttons at the top of the profile screen. Alphabet being learned: parent section → “Which letters to learn”.

Where is the parent section?

Tap the lock button and solve the task. Inside: progress, settings, worksheets to print, your own words.

There is no sound

Check the silent switch and the volume, then the sound settings in the parent section. Voice prompts use the system voices: you can download better ones in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.

How do I switch between print and cursive?

Parent section → profile settings → “Model script”. Cursive is available for the Russian alphabet.
