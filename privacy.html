Privacy Policy

ABC Tracing: Learn to Write (Letters & Numbers) · Effective date: 6 October 2026

We do not collect any data

Letters & Numbers requires no account and has no internet connection, advertising, analytics or tracking. Nothing is sent from the device to us or to anyone else.

What is stored on the device

Child profiles (a name or nickname and an avatar picked in the app), learning progress, the strokes a child writes on screen, settings, and — if a parent records them — voice recordings of the parent’s own words. All of this stays on the device only and is removed when the app is deleted.

Microphone

The microphone is used only in the parent section, behind a parental gate, when a parent records a word in their own voice. Recordings are stored on the device and are never transmitted.

Sharing and printing

A parent can print worksheets or share a child’s work or the error log from the parent section using the system share sheet. This happens only when the parent chooses to, and the app itself sends nothing.

Children

The app is designed for children and follows the rules for children’s apps: no ads, no purchases, no links out of the app, no third-party analytics.
